01Information We Collect

This app collects no personal information whatsoever.

We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, location data, device identifiers (including advertising IDs), or usage analytics. We do not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or calendar. We do not share or sell any data, because we hold none.

02Data Stored on Your Device

To let you resume your game, the app saves the following on your device only: game progress, language preference, display theme, and sound settings.

This data is stored using the operating system's local storage (UserDefaults on iOS, SharedPreferences on Android). It is never transmitted to any server and is not accessible to the developer. Deleting the app erases it completely.

03Network

The app performs no network communication. It works fully offline.

04Advertising and Purchases

The app contains no advertising. Any in-app purchase is processed by Apple or Google; the developer never receives your payment details.

05Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at any specific age group and, as stated above, collects no personal information from any user.

This app is an educational simulation game about how real estate investment works. It does not depict real properties, companies, or financial institutions, and it does not provide investment advice or solicitation.

Figures, yields, and financing terms in the game are simplified for learning purposes and do not represent actual market terms. Make real investment decisions at your own responsibility, consulting qualified professionals as needed. The developer accepts no liability for any loss arising from use of this app.
